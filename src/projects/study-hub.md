Study Hub is where I turn a semester of lecture decks, textbook chapters and transcripts into something I can actually learn from. It's a set of **offline-capable, self-contained study guides**, each built from scratch around one course, collected behind a single sign-in that keeps reading progress in sync across devices.

Each guide is written lesson by lesson instead of being summarised slide by slide. Concepts are rebuilt from first principles, with worked examples, diagrams, labs, flashcards and an exam drill at the end.

**Guides currently on the hub**

- **Big Data**: machine learning with big data, and graph analytics, as two tracks in one guide.
- **Interview Prep**: placement preparation for technical interviews, from scratch.
- **Social Network Analysis** (CSE 4464): networks, centrality and communities, lecture by lecture.
- **Ethical Hacking & Cyber Security** (CSE 4449): lessons, labs, flashcards and an exam drill.
- **Reinforcement Learning**: bandits, MDPs, dynamic programming and Monte Carlo methods, built from Sutton & Barto.

The guide sources live in private repositories, and the hub is the public front door.
