A physics-informed neural network that solves Maxwell's equations in potential form for the electromagnetic wakefield trailing a relativistic particle bunch. The d'Alembertian residual is enforced directly through automatic differentiation on Sobol-sampled collocation points, so the field is learned without ever meshing the domain.
